RGB Calc Pro

Privacy Policy  ·  Developer: UtilityNest  ·  Effective 13 October 2026

Short version: RGB Calc Pro collects no personal data, requests no device permissions, and never connects to the internet. Everything you type stays on your own phone.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or sensitive user data. The app has no account system, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics.

2. Permissions

The app requests no Android permissions. It has no access to the internet, your files, your location, your camera, your microphone, your contacts or your phone state.

3. Data stored on your device

To make the calculator convenient, the app saves two things in its own private storage on your device:

  • your calculation history, and
  • your sound preferences and the number you were last typing.

This information never leaves your device. It is not visible to us or to anyone else. You can erase all of it at any time using the Clear History button inside the app, or by uninstalling the app.

4. Third parties

The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics, tracking or social SDKs. No data is shared with anyone because no data is collected in the first place.

5. Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data whatsoever, it is safe for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect information from children, as we collect no information from anyone.

6. Security

Since no data is transmitted or stored off your device, there is no server or database holding your information that could be breached.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
